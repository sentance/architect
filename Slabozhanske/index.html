Reserve Modular Boiler House

Slobozhanske, UA

To ensure uninterrupted heat supply for the hospital of the Slobozhanske Village Council, LLC DB "Architect Expert", commissioned by LLC "Construction Company STEK" and funded by the World Health Organization, developed a project for a vital facility – a 1 MW block-modular boiler house with auxiliary buildings and structures for the Slobozhanske Central Hospital.
The boiler house, manufactured by LLC "ARES-UKRAINE", is supplied in full factory readiness and equipped with two solid-fuel fire-tube boilers Altep KVT Max, 600 kW each.

In addition to the modular boiler house itself, the following buildings and structures are located on the site:

  • Smoke stacks – 2 units;
  • External fuel storage bunker;
  • Bucket elevator with a small bunker for organizing and ensuring proper fuel supply;
  • Additional pellet storage bunker;
  • Generator housed under a metal frame canopy;
  • 3D mesh fence on metal posts, with gates and a wicket.
