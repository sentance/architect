Law Academy HALL 1,2

Dnipro, UA

In the first hall (A-7), according to the BTI passport, a redevelopment was carried out to create additional functional zones: a lobby, reception area, control area, a small conference hall for up to 15 people, and a food court zone.


The interior finishes include decorative plaster combined with wooden panels. A distinctive design feature is the lighting system – fixtures in the lobby, food court, and small conference hall create a unique atmosphere. The ceiling, made of Armstrong tiles combined with decorative lighting, gives the impression of being in outer space. Carpet flooring harmoniously complements the ceiling design. Storage rooms remain unchanged.


In the second hall (A-5), no redevelopment was performed; the geometric parameters of the premises remain unchanged. Renovation works included wall finishing with decorative plaster and painting, gypsum board ceilings concealing ventilation ducts, and carpet flooring.
