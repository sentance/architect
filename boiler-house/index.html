Reserve Modular Boiler House

Odessa, UA

To ensure uninterrupted heat supply for the hospital in Odesa, LLC DB "Architect Expert", commissioned by LLC "Construction Company STEK" and funded by the World Health Organization, developed a project for a vital facility – a 1 MW block-modular boiler house with auxiliary buildings and structures for the Odesa Regional Center for Socially Significant Diseases of the Odesa Regional Council.


The boiler house, manufactured by LLC "ARES-UKRAINE", is supplied in full factory readiness and equipped with two solid-fuel fire-tube boilers Altep KVT Max, 500 kW each.


The structural scheme of the boiler house is a module made of a metal frame, clad with profiled sheeting and insulated with mineral wool.

  • Smoke stacks – 2 units;
  • External fuel storage bunker;
  • External fuel storage bunker;
  • Bucket elevator with a small bunker for organizing and ensuring proper fuel supply;
  • Additional pellet storage bunker;
  • Generator housed under a metal frame canopy;
  • 3D mesh fence on metal posts, with gates and a wicket.

The total installed thermal capacity of the boiler house is 1.0 MW. The boiler house's thermal scheme provides for the preparation of heat for the consumer in the form of a heat carrier (water) for the heating system with a temperature schedule of 80-60°C. The volume of the external fuel storage bunker is 20 m³, designed for 7 days. The annual volume of fuel transportation is 465.23 tons/year, and the volume of ash transportation is 16.0 tons/year.

The main technical specifications of the modular boiler house include:

Height of the metal frame tower for the smoke stacks

23.45 meters

Built-up area of the boiler house complex and structures

97.91 m²

Potential heated area of hospital buildings and structures due to the boiler house capacity

up to 12,500 m²

Possible fuel types for the boiler house

pellets and firewood
